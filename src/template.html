Published research and our fusion model

Paper: statistical features selected with RF and classified with Gaussian SVM. Ours: AC + DC + history, a regularized healthy reference, and Logistic Regression.

Different tests: the paper uses a randomized segmented-data split; ours holds out complete tool-life cases. These scores do not establish which approach is better under identical conditions.
